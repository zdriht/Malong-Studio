Our Heritage

A living tradition, woven forward.

Malong Studio celebrates the artistry and everyday beauty of Filipino weaving. Inspired by the vivid textiles of Mindanao, each design honors the hands, stories, and communities behind the cloth while bringing heritage into modern life.

We believe a malong is more than fabric: it is a versatile expression of identity, craft, and connection.
